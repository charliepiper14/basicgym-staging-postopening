Quick answers

Before you join.

How much is it?

Four plans. Off Peak £17.99 and Plus £24.99 on a 12 month term, Off Peak £21.99 and Monthly £27.99 rolling. Joining fee £9.99 or £14.99, charged once and shown in the price.

Is there a contract?

Not on the monthly plans. They roll on and you can cancel any time. The 12 month plans run for 12 months, which is why they cost less.

What does off peak mean?

The Off Peak plans give you the gym between 10am and 4pm. Plus gives you 24 hour access. Classes are unlimited on every plan.

What’s included?

Every zone, 120 classes a week, showers and changing rooms, and 650 free parking spaces. No upsells.
