Before you join

Membership questions

Is there a joining fee?

Yes. £9.99 on the 12 month plans and £14.99 on the monthly plans, charged once. It is included in the total shown on each plan above, so the figure you see is the figure you pay on the day. The day pass has no joining fee.

Is there a contract?

Not on the monthly plans. They roll on automatically, and you can cancel whenever you like with 30 days’ notice. Our 12 month plans are fixed for 12 months, which is why they come at a lower monthly price.

What does off peak mean?

The Off Peak plans give you the gym between 10am and 4pm. Plus and Monthly give you 24 hour access. Classes are unlimited on every plan whichever you pick.

What is the SAVE50 code?

SAVE50 takes 50% off your first month on any of the four memberships. On the 12 month plans the joining fee is waived too. On the monthly plans the usual £14.99 joining fee applies. After that you pay your normal monthly price.

What is the Day Pass?

A single day’s access with no membership and no joining fee. £9.99, and it includes classes.

How do I pay?

Membership is collected monthly by Direct Debit. You set it up in a couple of minutes when you join.
