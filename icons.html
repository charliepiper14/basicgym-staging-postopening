Good to know

The small print,
kept short.

Do I need a big following?

No. Anyone can apply. We want people who love training and share it honestly, whatever their follower count.

How does the commission work?

You earn 30% of what each customer you refer pays in their first 3 months, after VAT, on any membership or coaching plan.

Do I have to label my posts?

Yes. Your membership and commission count as payment, so every post about BasicGym needs #ad at the start, or the platform’s paid partnership label. That’s the UK rule (ASA and CMA), and it protects you as much as us.

What happens after I apply?

We read every application. If it’s a fit, someone from the team gets in touch by phone or email.
