Before you join

Quick answers

How much is it?

Four plans. Off Peak £17.99 and Plus £24.99 on a 12 month term, Off Peak £21.99 and Monthly £27.99 rolling. Joining fee is £9.99 on the 12 month plans and £14.99 on monthly, charged once.

Is there a contract?

Not on the monthly plans, they roll on and you can cancel any time. The 12 month plans run for 12 months, which is why they cost less.

What does off peak mean?

The Off Peak plans give you the gym between 10am and 4pm. Plus gives you 24 hour access. Classes are unlimited on every plan.

What’s included?

Every zone, 120 classes a week, showers and changing rooms, and 650 free parking spaces.

Is parking free?

Completely free. 650 spaces, no app, no ticket and no charge to park.

Can I try before joining?

Two ways. A £9.99 day pass, or book a look round and someone will show you the place.
