Promotion terms

Promotion
terms.

The rules that apply to the Back to Basic sale and any other joining promotion we run.

Awaiting wording, and this one matters. The site advertises BACK26 as limited to 100 spaces. Under the DMCC Act 2024 a limited availability claim must be true, which means the club has to stop at 100 or the number on the site has to come down as they go. These terms are what evidences that, so they should be written now rather than after.

What the offer is

The discount, which plans it applies to, and whether the joining fee is included or still charged.

How many are available

The number of spaces on the offer, how they are counted, and what happens when they run out. If the cap is 100, say 100 and stop at 100.

How to claim it

The code, where it is entered, and by when.

What happens after the first month

The standard rate that applies from month two, stated in full.

Who can use it

New members only, or existing members too. One use per person.

Withdrawal

That the club may end or change a promotion, and what happens to anyone mid signup when it does.
