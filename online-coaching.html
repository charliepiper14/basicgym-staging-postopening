Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to be a BasicGym member?

No. Online personal training works wherever you train: at home, at BasicGym or at any other gym in the UK.

What equipment do I need?

Whatever you have. You tell us your kit when you start, and your plan is built around it, from bodyweight at home to a full gym.

Can I buy on my computer?

Yes, if you already have the BasicGym app. Log in to our web shop with the same details and pay by card. New to the app? Download it first to create your account.

What is the difference between the plans?

Training Scheme builds an AI plan around you. Online Personal Training adds a dedicated PT you can chat to and a monthly Zoom.

I have a health condition. Can I still join?

Every plan starts with a health check, and your plan is adapted to it. If you have a medical condition, check with your GP before starting a new training plan.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Start with Training Scheme, and step up when you want more support from a PT.
