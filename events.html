Meet The Movement Community

Community first. Movement always.

The Movement Community brings people across Nottingham together through movement. It’s about more than running: meeting like-minded people, getting active and building real connections along the way. Experienced runner, just starting out, or simply looking to meet new people, this is a community built around moving together.

No matter your pace. Everyone’s welcome.

Good to know

Before you
come along.

Where is it?

We meet at BasicGym Riverside, Riverside Retail Park, Nottingham NG2 1RU. There are 650 free parking spaces on site.

Is it free? Do I need to be a member?

It’s free, and open to members and non-members. There’s no cap on numbers, so bring a friend. Just register so we can send you the details.

Do I need to be a runner?

No. Whether you’re a beginner or a trained runner, everyone is welcome.

What should I bring?

Running shoes, gym kit, a water bottle and a layer for the run.
