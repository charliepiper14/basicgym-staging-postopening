Getting here

Three ways in.

We are in the main terrace at Riverside Retail Park, alongside B&Q, Boots, JD Sports and Next. Whichever way you come in, parking is free across the whole park.

A52

From the ring road

Come off at Clifton Boulevard and follow the signs for Riverside Retail Park. The entrance is on your left as you come into the park.

City centre

Ten minutes out

Head out on Queens Drive towards the retail park. Roughly ten minutes outside of rush hour.

A453

From the south

Quick from Clifton, Wilford and Ruddington. Fifteen minutes from Long Eaton and Beeston on a normal day.

Once you are here

Park, scan, train.

  1. Park anywhere on the parkAll 650 spaces are free and there is no barrier or ticket machine. The bays nearest the gym are along the main terrace.
  2. Come to the main entranceYou will see us in the terrace between the big-name stores. The door is on the front.
  3. Scan your QR codeYour membership QR lives in the BasicGym app and gets you through the turnstile. If it plays up, there is someone on the door.
