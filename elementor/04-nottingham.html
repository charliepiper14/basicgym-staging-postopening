Before you join

Quick answers

How much is it?

Four plans. Off Peak £17.99 and Plus £24.99 on a 12 month term, Off Peak £21.99 and Monthly £27.99 rolling. Joining fee is £9.99 on the 12 month plans and £14.99 on monthly, charged once.

Is there a contract?

Not on the monthly plans. They roll on automatically, and you can cancel whenever you like with 30 days’ notice. Our 12 month plans are fixed for 12 months, which is why they come at a lower monthly price.

What does off peak mean?

The Off Peak plans give you the gym between 10am and 4pm. Plus and Monthly give you 24 hour access. Classes are unlimited on every plan.

What’s included?

Every zone, 120 classes a week, showers and changing rooms, and 650 free parking spaces.

Is parking free?

Free parking for up to 4 hours between 6am and 8:30pm, or 24 hours when you register your vehicle on-site. 650 spaces.

Can I try before joining?

Two ways. A £9.99 day pass, or just walk in and ask for a look round.
