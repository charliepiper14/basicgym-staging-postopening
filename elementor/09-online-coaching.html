Questions

Before you start.

Do I need to be a BasicGym member?

No. Online coaching works wherever you train: at home, at BasicGym or at any other gym in the UK.

What equipment do I need?

Whatever you have. You tell us your kit when you start, and your plan is built around it, from bodyweight at home to a full gym.

How quickly do I get my plan?

Within 24 hours of finishing your health check and goals, straight into the app.

What is the difference between the plans?

Coach gives you a personal plan. Online Coaching adds a dedicated PT you can chat to and a monthly Zoom. Premium adds weekly check-ins and two Zooms a month.

I have a health condition. Can I still join?

Every plan starts with a health check, and your plan is adapted to it. If you have a medical condition, check with your GP before starting a new training plan.

Can I change plan later?

Yes. Start with Coach and step up to Online Coaching or Premium when you want more support. Draft: confirm how plan changes and cancelling work
